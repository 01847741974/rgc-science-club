Our Journey & Purpose

Established in 2023 through a series of events and challenges, RGC Science Club has reached its current position. We hold weekly meetings guided by our Chief Advisor, Dr. Md. Babul Hossain (Head of Zoology), and Co-Advisor, Md. Matiur Rahman (Associate Professor of Physics). Our main goal is to remove the fear of rigorous academics and create a genuine love for science. By working on practical projects, programming, robotics, and participating in Olympiads, our members develop essential skills in group work, connectivity, cooperation, and leadership.
